# Roadmap

## Near term

- add measured coil resistance and Hall-probe calibration data;
- test multiple target depths and non-axis-aligned fields;
- add thermal limits and driver-voltage constraints;
- package an interactive hosted demonstration.

## Research extensions

- experimentally validate field profiles on a benchtop array;
- optimise conductor paths rather than selecting from a fixed loop basis;
- add differentiable geometry optimisation;
- investigate robust min-max design and calibration-aware model predictive control;
- benchmark graph and neural-operator surrogates on larger geometry families.
