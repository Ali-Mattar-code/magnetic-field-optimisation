# Provenance and claim boundaries

The underlying MSc Physics research was conducted at University College London between September 2024 and September 2025. This repository was written in 2026 as a clean-room public implementation from the thesis specification and reported methodology.

The original research source was not simply republished. This avoids exposing draft artefacts and makes every public dependency, assumption, configuration, and test explicit.

## Labels

- **Reproduced**: generated by a committed script and configuration.
- **Historical**: stated in the dissertation; retained for research context.
- **Exploratory**: a hypothesis or preview requiring further validation.

Historical results are data, not defaults. Code must not silently insert them into reproduced summaries.
